# Classical Astrodienst parity contract

“Parity” means equivalent professional calculation and chart workflows after
removing modern bodies and modern-only doctrine. It does not mean copying
Astrodienst's prose, visual design, trademarks, accounts, shop, or editorial
horoscopes.

| Chart drawing PDF/data PDF | Print-ready report plus SVG, JSON, and CSV downloads |

## Deliberate exclusions

Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Chiron, asteroids, hypothetical planets, modern aspect
families, and modern rulership schemes cannot enter the public domain model.
Angles and calculated lots are retained as mathematical points; they are not
treated as planets.
